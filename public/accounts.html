How account rotation works

Each video is pinned to one account per platform — the least-used one at the moment its first part publishes — and every part of that video stays on that same account. Consecutive videos rotate across your connected accounts automatically. Reconnecting an account that is already linked just refreshes its tokens and doesn't use up a slot.

The "posts" setting is each account's own rhythm. On Default schedule the account follows the global timeline (part 1 when processing finishes, each next part after the configured interval). Pick a cadence instead — say every 30 min on X and every 2 hours on YouTube — and that account works through its queue at exactly that pace, one post per window, always in part order. Posts are never skipped, only paced.
