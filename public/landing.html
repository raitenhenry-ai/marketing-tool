How it works

Three steps. The first one is the only one that needs you.

Upload your video

Drop in a talking-head video, a stream VOD, a tutorial — anything long-form. Pick auto-publish or download-only.

Clint does the work

Clipping, captions, titles, hashtags and scheduling happen automatically. Review the clips, tweak the cuts, or just let it run.

Posted everywhere, tracked

Clips go out across your connected accounts on schedule, and the analytics flow back in — with links to every live post.

Free while in early access

We're onboarding creators in batches while we scale up processing capacity. Grab a spot — early accounts keep their perks.
