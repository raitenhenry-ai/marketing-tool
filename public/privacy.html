Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2026

This policy describes what data this content-publishing tool (the "Service"), operated by clint.build, collects and how it is used.

Data we store

  • Videos and clips you upload, and the short clips generated from them, stored on the Service's server until deleted by you or by the configured retention policy.
  • Social account credentials: when you connect a social account via OAuth, the Service stores the access/refresh tokens the platform issues, along with the account's name and identifier. The Service never sees or stores your platform passwords.
  • Publishing records and statistics: which clips were posted where and when, and public engagement metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) retrieved from the platforms' APIs.

How data is used

Stored data is used solely to operate the Service: processing videos, publishing clips to the accounts you connected, and showing you your own publishing history and analytics. Audio from uploaded videos may be sent to OpenAI's API to generate subtitles and post text. Data is never sold or shared with third parties beyond the platforms you explicitly connect and the processing described here.

Data deletion

Disconnecting a social account deletes its stored tokens immediately. Deleting a video removes the video, its clips and its files from the server. You can also revoke the Service's access at any time from each platform's own security settings, which invalidates the stored tokens.

Third parties

Publishing and statistics involve the APIs of the platforms you connect (YouTube/Google, Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads), TikTok, X, Pinterest, LinkedIn), each governed by its own privacy policy. Google user data is used only to upload videos to and read statistics from your own YouTube channel, and is never transferred to others or used for advertising.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: via www.clint.build.
